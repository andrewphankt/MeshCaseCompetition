This is the problem the whole pitch answers. Read the headline, let the 84 dots fill: almost every developer uses AI. Then click: only 17 in 100 say AI agents made their team work better together. AI made building alone easy; building together is still broken. Why now: this year, AI coding tools went from one person to whole teams.
Where people get stuck

People with ideas can get an AI to write code. Then they get stuck.

  1. 1You don't know the words.
  2. 2You can't follow what your AI, or your team's, just did.
  3. 3You don't know who could help.
This is who Mesh is for: people with ideas, not engineers. Pause after each line.

Layer 2 · Easy to start · Clickable prototype

Practice first. Tooltips until you're ready.

Our goalA first-timer's first save in under 5 minutes, with no help messages.
Two ways we make it easy to start:
  • Training mode: tooltips in the live room, which you turn off when you're ready.
  • Practice room: a separate safe copy with practice teammates. Nothing touches the real project.
  • A one-minute “Start here” tour.
  • Pasted passwords get blocked.
